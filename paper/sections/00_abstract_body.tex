\shap{} and \lime{} dominate tabular explainability yet frequently disagree on the same
prediction. We present a pre-registered, leakage-safe benchmark comparing \shap{}, \lime{},
rank-level fusion rules, and a random control across six classifiers and three public
clinical datasets (\NumCells{} cells, \NumExplained{} instances, eleven hypotheses tested
at \rev{Holm-adjusted $p_{\text{adj}}<0.05$}), \rev{complemented by post-review validations
spanning five \lime{} sampling budgets ($500$--$10{,}000$), on-manifold conditional $k$-NN
and retraining-based (\textsc{roar}) fidelity, adaptive fusion, and two larger external
clinical datasets (up to $5{,}000$ rows, $30$ features).} \shap{} and \lime{} agree
substantially (median Spearman $\rho=\MedianRho{}$, top-5 Jaccard \MedianJaccardFive{},
\rev{$p_{\text{adj}}<10^{-3}$} in \HOneRejected{}/\HOneTotal{} cells) yet differ on one
top-five feature, agreeing least on \rev{feature-redundant} CKD ($\rho=\RhoCkd{}$).
Three-way stability decomposition shows determinism is not robustness: \textsc{TreeSHAP}'s
re-run variance is zero, yet retraining drops agreement to $\RetrainShap{}$.
\rev{Increasing \lime{}'s budget $20\times$ cuts re-run CV from $\LimeCvFiveH{}$ to
$\LimeCvTenK{}$, but \aopc{} plateaus at $\LimeAopcTwoHalfK{}$ below \shap{} ($\AopcShap{}$)
and \lime{} stays $\ge\TreeRatioFiveH{}\times$ slower on trees while beating permutation
\shap{} on stacking. \shap{} leads local median, marginal, and conditional $k$-NN fidelity
(\CondShapWins{}/18 primary, \ScaleCondWins{}/12 external, $p_{\text{adj}}<0.05$), whereas
global \textsc{roar} retraining removes their gap ($p=\RoarAucP{}$). Static,
calibration-tuned, and $R^2$-adaptive fusion win \FusionMaxWins{}/18 cells due to minimal
oracle headroom ($\OracleHeadroomMed{}$ \aopc{}).} Every result regenerates from code.
