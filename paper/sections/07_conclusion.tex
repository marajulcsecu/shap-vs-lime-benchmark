\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Across \NumCells{} model--dataset cells, \shap{} and \lime{} agree on feature rankings
only moderately (median $\rho = \MedianRho{}$), and \shap{} is the more faithful,
cheaper, and more perturbation-robust of the two wherever a tree backend exists.
Explanation stability separates into three sources that are routinely conflated, and
the one that survives a deterministic explainer --- variance from retraining the model
--- is the one that a determinism guarantee cannot address. Rank-level fusion of the
two did not clear the pre-registered bar of beating the better of them, which follows
from their moderate agreement and \shap{}'s consistent advantage.

The protocol, code, data, and every figure and table in this paper regenerate from a
single command, against hypotheses frozen in version control before any result existed.
