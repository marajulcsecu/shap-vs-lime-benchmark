\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Across \NumCells{} model--dataset cells, \shap{} and \lime{} agree substantially on
feature rankings (median $\rho = \MedianRho{}$) without being interchangeable: about a
third of each top-five list differs. Where a tree backend exists \shap{} is the more
faithful, roughly \SpeedFactor{}$\times$ cheaper, and markedly more perturbation-robust.
Explanation stability separates into three sources, and the one a deterministic explainer
cannot address --- variance from retraining the model --- is the one that persists.
Rank-level fusion did not clear the pre-registered bar of beating the better of the two in
any cell, which follows from that combination: substantial agreement leaves little
complementary signal, and a consistent advantage can only be diluted by averaging.
