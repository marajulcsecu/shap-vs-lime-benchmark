\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\shap{} computes Shapley attributions, with \textsc{TreeSHAP} enabling polynomial-time
evaluation on trees~\cite{lundberg2017unified,lundberg2020local}; \lime{} fits a weighted
local ridge surrogate over perturbed samples~\cite{ribeiro2016why}. \rev{Han et
al.~\cite{han2022which} unified both as local function approximations differing in
neighbourhood and loss, and Krishna et al.~\cite{krishna2024disagreement} documented the
resulting practitioner disagreement problem. OpenXAI~\cite{agarwal2022openxai} standardised
evaluation axes, whereas clinical tabular comparisons and healthcare \textsc{xai}
studies~\cite{salih2024perspective,jahin2025ckd}} typically omit random controls,
output-space checks, and pre-registered hypothesis tests.

\rev{On stability, Garreau and von Luxburg~\cite{garreau2020explaining} showed finite
sampling and bandwidth choices inject variance into \lime{}, and Visani et
al.~\cite{visani2022statistical} formalised statistical stability indices for \lime{}},
while Alvarez-Melis and Jaakkola~\cite{alvarezmelis2018robustness} introduced local
Lipschitz robustness. \rev{Yet tabular benchmarks rarely separate re-run, perturbation, and
retraining variance alongside \lime{}'s sampling budget.}

Faithfulness tests delete ranked features and measure prediction drop via
\aopc{}~\cite{samek2017evaluating}, comprehensiveness, and sufficiency, \rev{unified as
removal-based explanations by Covert et al.~\cite{covert2021explaining}. Unconditional
median or marginal replacement breaks feature
correlations~\cite{hooker2019benchmark,aas2021explaining}, reflecting the
\emph{true-to-the-model} vs.\ \emph{true-to-the-data} split~\cite{chen2020truemodel};
\textsc{roar}~\cite{hooker2019benchmark} and consistent removal~\cite{rong2022consistent}
retrain models to remove off-manifold bias.} Finally, explanation
aggregation~\cite{bhatt2020aggregating}, reciprocal-rank
fusion~\cite{cormack2009reciprocal}, and \rbo{}~\cite{webber2010similarity} motivate our
leakage-safe evaluation of \rev{static and adaptive} rank fusion.
