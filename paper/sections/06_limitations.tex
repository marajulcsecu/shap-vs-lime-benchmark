\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:limitations}

We state these before a reader has to raise them. \textbf{Three datasets, all small and
all clinical:} the per-cell tests have real power, but generalisation across \emph{datasets}
rests on three points, and the difficulty gradient is a design compensation rather than a
substitute for breadth. \textbf{Perturbation baselines are off-manifold:} median
replacement creates rows the data-generating process would not
produce~\cite{hooker2019benchmark}; a marginal-draw variant did not reverse the ordering,
but both share an independence assumption. \textbf{The plausibility reference sets are not
ground truth:} compiled from cited literature and frozen before any result existed, they
remain incomplete, non-causal, and shaped by what their sources chose to measure, which is
why \RQ{5} is corroborating evidence only. \textbf{Retraining stability covers three of six
models}, restricted in advance on compute grounds. \textbf{Timing is single-machine:} the
\SpeedFactor{}$\times$ gap reflects a structural difference, but its magnitude is
hardware-specific. \textbf{No human evaluation:} every metric here is a property of the
model--explanation pair. Finally, following Rudin~\cite{rudin2019stop}, a faithful post-hoc
explanation of a black box is not an interpretable model.

\textbf{The additivity gate failed for one cell, and we report it rather than widen the
gate.} In PIMA/LightGBM, \AdditivityWorstOutliers{} of 150 explained instances reconstruct
\code{predict\_proba} to worse than the $5\times10^{-3}$ tolerance (worst
\AdditivityWorstMax{}) against a cell median of $1.1\times10^{-9}$, so the pre-registered
rule is not satisfied. The discrepancy is insensitive to background size and we did not
attribute it; the obvious candidate, coincidence with a median-imputed value, was tested and
rejected. Our two pre-registered options were to force that cell into log-odds space, which would
remove it from every cross-explainer comparison, or to widen the gate. We did neither:
widening a gate after seeing the result it fails is what pre-registration exists to
prevent. The cell is retained, the failure stated, and every tally additionally reported
with the cell excluded; no decision in Table~\ref{tab:hypotheses} changes.
