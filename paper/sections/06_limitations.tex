\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:limitations}

\textbf{Dataset scope:} our primary pre-registered benchmark rests on three small clinical
datasets; \rev{while the $12$-cell external validation on BCWD ($d=30$) and CDC Diabetes
($n=5{,}000$) substantially strengthens generality, extending to non-clinical and very high
dimensionality ($d>100$) regimes remains future work. \textbf{Dependence assumptions:}
conditional $k$-NN preserves empirical joint structure locally but relies on Euclidean
neighbourhoods in standardised space; \textsc{roar} avoids off-manifold bias completely
but evaluates global rather than per-instance attributions.} \textbf{Scope bounds:}
retraining stability covers the three tree ensembles (protocol R3), timing is
single-machine, and no human-subject evaluation is performed.

\textbf{Disclosed additivity gate failure (protocol risk R1):} in PIMA/LightGBM,
\AdditivityWorstOutliers{}/150 instances exceed the $5\times 10^{-3}$ probability-space
additivity gate (worst \AdditivityWorstMax{}, cell median $1.1\times 10^{-9}$). Rather than
widen the gate post-hoc or drop the cell into log-odds space, we retain and disclose it and
verify in \file{results/stats_sensitivity_r1.csv} that excluding PIMA/LightGBM alters zero
hypothesis decisions in Table~\ref{tab:hypotheses}.
