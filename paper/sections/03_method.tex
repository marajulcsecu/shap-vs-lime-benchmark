\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Datasets, models, and separated tracks}\label{sec:method-data}
Our pre-registered primary benchmark spans three public, de-identified clinical datasets
vendored with verified SHA-256 checksums: PIMA Indian Diabetes (UCI/NIDDK, $768\times 8$,
$34.9\,\%$ positive), UCI Heart Disease Cleveland (UCI~\#45, $303\times 13$, $45.9\,\%$),
and UCI Chronic Kidney Disease (UCI~\#336, $400\times 24$, $62.5\,\%$). They form a
difficulty gradient (nested-CV ROC-AUC \AucPima{}, \AucHeart{}, \AucCkd{}). Biologically
impossible zeros in PIMA (glucose, blood pressure, skin thickness, insulin, BMI) are
recoded as missing while retaining legitimate zeros in \emph{pregnancies}; Heart targets
are binarised at $>0$ with nominal codes one-hot encoded. \rev{To test generalisation
beyond small cohorts (Post-Review Amendment A8), we add an \emph{external scalability check}
on two larger or higher-dimensional clinical benchmarks: Breast Cancer Wisconsin Diagnostic
(BCWD, UCI~\#17, $569\times 30$, $37.3\,\%$ malignant) and a seeded class-stratified cohort
of CDC Diabetes Health Indicators (UCI~\#891, $5{,}000\times 21$ from $N=253{,}680$,
$13.9\,\%$ positive).}

Six classifiers span linear, single-tree, bagged, boosted, and stacked families: logistic
regression, decision tree, random forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and a logistic-regression
stacking ensemble over the three tree ensembles. \textbf{Track A} estimates predictive
performance via nested $5\times 3$ stratified CV with all preprocessing inside the
pipeline. \textbf{Track B} fixes those inner-loop hyperparameters, refits once on a
stratified $80/20$ split (seed $42$), and runs every explainer on up to $150$ stratified
test instances per cell ($150$ PIMA, $61$ Heart, $80$ CKD; \NumExplained{} primary
instances, \rev{plus $114$ BCWD and $150$ CDC instances per model externally}). No
synthetic oversampling is applied because altering the training manifold would confound
both explainers~\rev{\cite{niaz2022imbalance}}; \code{class\_weight=balanced} is used where
supported.

\subsection{Explainers, budget sweep, and metrics}\label{sec:method-metrics}
\shap{} uses exact \textsc{TreeExplainer} for decision tree and random forest,
interventional probability-space \textsc{TreeExplainer} ($50$ seeded stratified real
training rows) for XGBoost and LightGBM, and model-agnostic \textsc{PermutationExplainer}
($10$ passes) for logistic regression and stacking. \lime{} uses
\code{LimeTabularExplainer} with one-hot columns declared categorical,
\rev{\code{discretize\_continuous=True}, default kernel width $0.75\sqrt{d_{\text{enc}}}$,
and primary budget $N_{\text{samples}}=5{,}000$. To evaluate sensitivity to sampling budget
fairly, Amendment A8 sweeps $N_{\text{samples}}\in\{500, 1{,}000, 2{,}500, 5{,}000,
10{,}000\}$ across all \NumCells{} primary cells, recording runtime, re-run stability,
\aopc{}, and agreement with \shap{}.} A uniformly random ranking ($20$ draws) is the
mandatory control.

Let $\phi(x)\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ denote aggregated original-feature attributions and $\pi(x)$
their descending $|\phi(x)|$ ranking. \textbf{Agreement (\RQ{1})} measures local and global
Spearman $\rho$, Kendall $\tau_b$, top-$k$ Jaccard, and floor-normalised \rbo{} ($p=0.90$;
raw floors \RboFloorEight{} at $d=8$ to \RboFloorTwentyFour{} at $d=24$). \rev{To diagnose
disagreement drivers, we compute each dataset's correlation effective-rank ratio
($\exp(H(\tilde{\lambda}))/d$) and count of features achieving univariate ROC-AUC
$\ge 0.80$.} \textbf{Stability (\RQ{2})} decomposes into: (2a) \emph{re-run variance}
across $R=10$ seeds (CV and repeat-pair $\rho$); (2b) \emph{local robustness} via local
Lipschitz $\hat{L}(x)=\max_{x'}\lVert\phi(x)-\phi(x')\rVert_2/\lVert x-x'\rVert_2$ over
$m=5$ Gaussian neighbours ($0.1\sigma$)~\cite{alvarezmelis2018robustness}; and (2c)
\emph{retraining stability} as cross-refit global Spearman $\rho$ across $B=10$ stratified
bootstrap resamples of the three tree ensembles. \textbf{Fidelity (\RQ{3})} evaluates
deletion $\aopc{}=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}[f_c(x)-f_c(x_{\setminus\text{top-}k})]$
($K=\min(d,10)$) alongside insertion AUC, comprehensiveness, and
sufficiency under three local baselines: primary training median,
$5$-draw independent marginal, \rev{and \emph{on-manifold conditional $k$-NN replacement}
(replacing deleted columns $S_k$ jointly from the $M=10$ nearest training rows matched on
the retained columns $\bar{S}_k$). To eliminate fixed-model perturbation bias altogether,
we further run global \textsc{roar}~\cite{hooker2019benchmark}: removing the top
$10,20,30,40,50\,\%$ globally ranked features and retraining each pipeline from scratch
under $5$-fold CV (mean $\Delta\text{ROC-AUC}$ and $\Delta\text{Log-Loss}$ vs.\ five random
permutations).} \textbf{Cost (\RQ{4})} is single-threaded wall-clock seconds
(\code{OMP\_NUM\_THREADS=1}). \textbf{Plausibility (\RQ{5})} is top-5 overlap with a
frozen literature reference set (corroborating only).

\subsection{Static, calibration-tuned, and adaptive rank fusion}\label{sec:method-hybrid}
Given normalised ranks $r^{A}, r^{B}\in[0,1]^d$ of $|\phi^{\shap}|$ and $|\phi^{\lime}|$,
our pre-registered study tests three rules: \emph{mean-rank (Borda)}
$r=\tfrac{1}{2}(r^{A}+r^{B})$, \emph{reciprocal-rank fusion} (\textsc{rrf})
$s_j=(60+r^{A}_j)^{-1}+(60+r^{B}_j)^{-1}$~\cite{cormack2009reciprocal}, and
\emph{proportional fidelity-weighted fusion} $r=w\,r^{A}+(1-w)\,r^{B}$ with
$w=\aopc{}^{\text{cal}}_{\shap}/(\aopc{}^{\text{cal}}_{\shap}+\aopc{}^{\text{cal}}_{\lime})$
fitted on a disjoint $30\,\%$ calibration split per cell and tested on the remaining
$70\,\%$. \rev{Because proportional normalisation naturally stays near $0.50$, Amendment A8
adds three stronger calibration-fitted variants on the exact same $30/70$ split:
(i)~\emph{grid-tuned weight} $w^*\in\{0,0.05,\dots,1\}$ maximising calibration \aopc{};
(ii)~\emph{instance-level \lime{} $R^2$-adaptive fusion}
$w(x)=\operatorname{clip}(w_0-\gamma z(R^2_{\lime}(x)),0,1)$ using \lime{}'s inference-time
local surrogate fit $R^2$ (\code{exp.score}); and (iii)~\emph{$R^2$ selective gating}
(routing to \lime{} when $R^2_{\lime}(x)\ge\tau^*$, else \shap{}). Every rule is tested
against both the calibration-selected best single explainer (deployable bar) and the
per-instance oracle $\max(\aopc{}_{\shap}(x),\aopc{}_{\lime}(x))$.}

\subsection{Four methodological traps avoided}\label{sec:pitfalls}
(1)~\emph{Same output space:} \shap{} and \lime{} both explain predicted probability,
verified via $|\text{base}+\sum_j\phi_j - f_1(x)|$ (median error \AdditivityMedianMax{};
one cell, PIMA/LightGBM, has \AdditivityWorstOutliers{}/150 outliers above $5\times 10^{-3}$,
disclosed in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). (2)~\emph{Real background rows:} $k$-means
centroids create fractional one-hot values that broke LightGBM additivity
($9.9\times 10^{-3}$ vs.\ $7\times 10^{-9}$ on real rows). (3)~\emph{One-hot group
aggregation:} attributions are summed and features perturbed at original-feature
granularity. (4)~\emph{Random control:} every fidelity score is benchmarked against random
rankings.

\subsection{Statistics}\label{sec:method-stats}
Eleven hypotheses were pre-registered in version control before any result existed
(Table~\ref{tab:hypotheses}). All tests are non-parametric (Wilcoxon signed-rank,
Mann--Whitney U, or permutation Spearman) at \rev{$\alpha=0.05$, with Holm--Bonferroni
adjustment ($p_{\text{adj}}<0.05$)} within each family, rank-biserial effect sizes $r$, and
$95\,\%$ percentile bootstrap intervals.
