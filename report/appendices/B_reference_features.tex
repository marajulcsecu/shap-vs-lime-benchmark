\chapter{Pre-Registered Plausibility Reference Sets}\label{app:refsets}

These sets were compiled from the sources below and committed to version control
\emph{before} any explanation was computed, which is what makes \RQ{5} a pre-registered
check rather than a post-hoc rationalisation. They are not ground truth: they are
incomplete, they are not causal, and they reflect what their source literature chose to
measure. A low overlap score is as consistent with an incomplete reference set as with a
poor explanation.

\section{Sources}
\textbf{PIMA Indian Diabetes} --- plasma glucose concentration is the diagnostic basis
for type 2 diabetes, and BMI and age are the most consistently reported risk factors in
this cohort. Compiled from the American Diabetes Association standards of care and the
dataset's originating study~\cite{smith1988using}.

\textbf{Heart Disease (Cleveland)} --- chest-pain type, exercise-induced ST depression,
maximum achieved heart rate, and the vessel and thalassemia indicators dominate the
Cleveland literature, beginning with the study that produced the
data~\cite{detrano1989international}.

\textbf{Chronic Kidney Disease} --- serum creatinine and haemoglobin are central to CKD
staging under the KDIGO guideline; albumin, specific gravity, and packed cell volume are
the next most frequently reported. Compiled from the KDIGO clinical practice guideline
and the dataset's documentation~\cite{rubini2015ckd}.
