# Code of Conduct

This is a small academic project by four undergraduate authors and their supervisor. The
expectations are simple.

**Be straightforward.** Disagree with the work, not the person. Say what you think is
wrong and why, with a pointer to the code, data, or protocol section that supports it.

**Be accurate.** Do not overstate a result, and do not describe a finding as established
when it is not. The paper's limitations section exists because the study has real limits;
treat them as facts rather than as weaknesses to hide.

**Credit properly.** Author contributions are recorded in `docs/CONTRIBUTIONS.md`. If your
contribution changes, update it. Prior work is cited; nothing here claims priority for
explanation aggregation or for any metric we did not invent.

**No harassment.** Personal attacks, discriminatory language, and unwelcome attention are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or commit messages.

Concerns can be raised with the repository owner or with the project supervisor,
Dr. M. Jamshed Alam Patwary, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, University of
Chittagong.
